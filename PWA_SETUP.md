Your Smart Farming Assistant is now a **fully functional Progressive Web App** that can be installed on mobile devices and work offline!

### PWA Features Enabled:
- ✅ **Service Worker** registered for offline functionality
- ✅ **Web App Manifest** with app metadata
- ✅ **Install Prompt** for "Add to Home Screen"

## 📱 How to Install on Mobile

### Android (Chrome/Edge):
1. Open **http://192.168.1.9:5173** on your phone
2. Tap the **menu (⋮)** in the browser
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
4. Tap **"Install"** on the prompt
5. App icon appears on your home screen! 🎉

### iOS (Safari):
1. Open **http://192.168.1.9:5173** in Safari
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow)
3. Scroll and select **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. App icon appears on your home screen! 🎉

### Desktop (Chrome/Edge):
1. Open **http://localhost:5173**
2. Look for **install icon** in the address bar
3. Click **"Install SmartFarm AI"**
4. App opens in its own window!

## 🔧 Testing PWA Locally

### Browser DevTools (F12):
1. **Application Tab** → **Manifest**
   - Should show: "SmartFarm AI — Smart Farming Assistant"
   - Theme color: #2E7D32
   - Icons: 192x192, 512x512

2. **Application Tab** → **Service Workers**
   - Should show registered service worker at `/sw.js`
   - Status: Activated and running

3. **Network Tab** → **Offline Mode**
   - Toggle offline mode
   - App should still load (cached assets)

### Lighthouse PWA Audit:
1. Open **DevTools** → **Lighthouse**
2. Select **"Progressive Web App"**
3. Click **"Generate Report"**
4. Should pass all PWA criteria! ✅
